# Legal and provenance

F0X is an unofficial community integration. It does not include or redistribute
F-Zero X, Nintendo assets, ROMs, Expansion Kit disks, IPL dumps, game-derived
archives, saves, credentials, provisioning profiles, or signing keys.

G-Diffuser, libultraship, and Torch are MIT-licensed at the pinned revisions;
the pinned F-Zero X and Expansion Kit matching-decomp repositories declare
CC0-1.0. Their licenses do not grant rights to Nintendo-owned game data.

Every public source, release artifact, and Git staging set must be checked for
prohibited game data and signing material.
