Follow-up cartridge sequence parity and clean scripted route
- Source audit found the port started `Audio_SESeqStart()` early from
  `Audio_Init()` while suppressing the original cartridge
  `Audio_GuitarSeqStart()` in `Game_ThreadEntry()`. The original call initializes
  sequence player 1 and then the SE sequence player at the intended boot point;
  those paths are not interchangeable.
- Cartridge builds now preserve the original unconditional call. Only Expansion
  Kit builds apply the live-drive guard required for DD-only `MEDIUM_LBA` data.
