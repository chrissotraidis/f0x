F0X macOS duplicate-bundle and dense presentation evidence
Date: 2026-08-12
Host: Apple Silicon macOS

Contradictory state
-------------------
The owner continued to see rapid flashing and no F0X interface while direct
inspection of the current sealed product showed F0X Home and stable gameplay.

Two generated macOS products were present in the same build directory:

  build/macos-f0x-bundle/port/F0X.app
  build/macos-f0x-bundle/port/G-Diffuser.app

Both Info.plists declared CFBundleIdentifier=com.chrissotraidis.f0x and
CFBundleName=F0X. Their executables were different:

  F0X.app/Contents/MacOS/F0X
  SHA-256 6301135301988cfdcb4d60ba5d40151b0dfc5d99195fce660e09e1169365fadf
  contained string: F0X Home

  G-Diffuser.app/Contents/MacOS/G-Diffuser
  SHA-256 984fde896205a593c1b5f4e75d2c87221e8bd21209410d67b0ffc11bf289978d
  did not contain string: F0X Home

Spotlight returned both paths for the bundle identifier. This does not prove
which path the owner launched, but it is a concrete mechanism for macOS to
resolve F0X to the obsolete pre-Home, pre-presentation-fix product.

Dense capture results
---------------------
The current product ran scripts/macos-metal-stability.gdx through a live race.
A direct 8-second window video analyzed with
scripts/analyze-metal-capture.swift produced:

  frames=462 duration_seconds=8.000
  mean_luma_min=97.30 mean_luma_max=98.05
  max_black_fraction=0.0422 near_black_frames=0
  max_adjacent_difference=1.35 brightness_jumps=0

The obsolete bundle was removed, current F0X.app was explicitly registered,
and /usr/bin/open launched that exact app into the visible Home surface. A new
direct window video produced:

  frames=544 duration_seconds=9.500
  mean_luma_min=8.20 mean_luma_max=8.22
  max_black_fraction=0.6611 near_black_frames=0
  max_adjacent_difference=0.13 brightness_jumps=0

The dark Home background explains its low mean luma; the signal remained stable
and never crossed the analyzer's near-black mean-frame threshold.

Durable regression
------------------
The macOS packaged build now makes G-Diffuser depend on
gdx_remove_legacy_macos_bundle, which deletes only the exact generated path
port/G-Diffuser.app before F0X.app is linked.

A fake legacy bundle and marker were created, the cleanup target was run, and
the directory was absent afterward (`legacy_cleanup_target=pass`). Ninja's
target query showed F0X's linker has an order-only dependency on
port/gdx_remove_legacy_macos_bundle.

The complete packaged rebuild succeeded after adding the cleanup. The current
app passed strict deep code-signature validation and plist lint. The maintained
G-Diffuser patch passed its reverse-apply check.

Boundary
--------
Current F0X.app is stable under these dense signals and the ambiguous legacy
product is gone. Owner confirmation after launching this corrected product is
still required; this evidence does not erase or contradict what was seen before
the duplicate-bundle cause was removed.
