F0X iOS touch visual, Input Editor, audio, and diagnostics acceptance
Date: 2026-08-13

Scope and privacy
-----------------
- One booted Simulator and one F0X process were used at a time.
- Live screenshots contained authorized game imagery and remain local/untracked.
- This artifact records only ROM-safe behavioral and build facts.
- No physical Apple device or valid signing identity was available.

Input Editor and touch visuals
------------------------------
- iPad Pro 11-inch (M5), landscape:
  - Input Editor occupied the safe viewport instead of a stale narrow desktop rectangle.
  - Buttons rendered beside D-Pad and Analog Stick in balanced primary columns.
  - Mapping chips were aligned by label/mapping columns.
  - Analog Options, Additional Right Stick, Rumble, Gyro, and LEDs remained reachable.
  - Gameplay controls and the permanent menu affordance were absent while editing.
- iPhone 17 Pro, landscape:
  - Settings exposed Open Input Editor above its description.
  - The primary editor layout collapsed to one column with safe margins.
  - Gameplay controls and the permanent menu affordance were absent while editing.
  - Closing the editor returned to Settings; closing Settings restored the neutral gameplay overlay.
- Compact gameplay controls use concise N64 glyphs while AX labels/hints preserve F-Zero action semantics.
- Phone rails and D-pad no longer collide; the analog knob is round; the duplicate Settings reset row is gone.

Audio and diagnostics
---------------------
- Pinned HarkinianPad reference decision was adopted at the libultraship layer:
  CoreAudio remains macOS-only and iOS defaults to SDL.
- Rebuilt iPhone 17 Pro Simulator launch logged:
  SDL Audio initialized: 2 channels, 32000 Hz
  dedicated audio thread started (DesiredBuffered=4096)
  live Audio_ThreadEntry wakeups and BGM synthesis
- Settings search for diagnostic exposed Share Diagnostic Log.
- Activating the action presented UIActivityViewController for a 2 KB text report.
- The report contained platform/OS/arch, Fast3D/Metal device, window/refresh,
  data classifications and sizes, controller/touch state, scheduler, active audio producer,
  timing/game mode, and a bounded warning tail.
- Audit found no absolute private path, ROM/save content, or signing material.
