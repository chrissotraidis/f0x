Negative instrumentation finding
--------------------------------
MallocStackLogging is incompatible with this runtime's custom ucontext fiber
stack. It causes an independent pre-target fault in thread_stack_pcs while a
fiber-originated console log allocates a C++ string. It was therefore excluded
from the valid graphics soak; guard edges and allocation scribbling remained
enabled.
